What's your virtue?

A mentor I looked up to once asked me what my virtue was, what drives me when nothing else does. I said learning, so quickly it almost didn't sound true.

I sat with that answer for months before realising it really is at my core. When I look at what actually makes me happy, it comes down to a few simple things: learning something new, being part of a community, and helping that community grow.

That, along with my love for technology and psychology, is what led me to cybersecurity itself: the habit of taking a system apart to understand it, then putting it back together a little harder to break. Everything else I have picked up since, AI included, still comes back to that same way of thinking.
