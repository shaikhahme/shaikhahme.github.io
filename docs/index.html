What's your virtue?

A mentor I looked up to once asked me what my virtue was, what drives me when nothing else does. I said learning, so quickly it almost didn't sound true. I sat with that answer for months before realising it really is at my core. When I look at what actually makes me happy, it comes down to a few simple things: learning something new, being part of a community, and helping that community grow. That, along with my love for technology and psychology, is what led me to cybersecurity, and especially to where it meets artificial intelligence. These days, AI alignment and safety is what holds my attention the most, since it feels like a problem that will only matter more to all of us.
